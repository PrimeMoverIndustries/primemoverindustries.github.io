Grand strategy meets industrial tycoon. Found an electrical company in 1890 and invent, build, trade and litigate your way through an alternate history of American industry. Can you own the century before Edison, Westinghouse and Morgan do?

The Laboratory

Seven fields, from the dynamo onward

Electrical, electronics, materials, chemical, manufacturing, transport and computing. Every claim is tried in the field, with money and engineers at stake, and a failure costs both.

The Big Board

Quoted in eighths, as it was

Buy into the railroads and the trusts, watch a panic take them down, and take your own company public when the country is ready to believe in it.

The Country

A continent that keeps time

Seasons turn, towns grow with the census, and the lights come on where the wires reach. Buy land where it is cheap or build where the capital is, and pay for the difference.

The Papers

The Courier is watching

The New-York Courier reports what you did and what the country made of it, from the founding to the crashes and the wars.

The Eras

The game changes with the times

The office you run your company from is refitted as the century turns: khaki when the Great War reaches the papers, prussian blue after the armistice, olive drab for the second war, phosphor green in the fifties. The Exchange's chart changes with it, from a brass integrator to a pen on a cord, an oscilloscope's beam, and at last the terminal's red and green candles.

The Titans

They have tempers, and memories

Fifteen figures of the age, each painted for the game in every mood your dealings can put them in. Hover to please them; tap to see the rest.
